    Questions

    Does anything go to the cloud?

    No. Screenshots, window titles, your timeline and the AI summaries stay on your computer — the AI model runs locally. RESUME only goes online to download the model once (about 1.3 GB), to check your license key about once a day, and to check for updates.

    Is this employee monitoring?

    No. There are no accounts, no admin and no way to send your data to anyone else. RESUME only records when you press Start, shows a recording light the whole time, and lets you pause, exclude apps or delete anything.

    Does it record my keystrokes or microphone?

    Never. It notes which app and window are in front and takes screenshots at the interval you choose (10, 15 or 30 seconds). Idle detection only checks how long since you last touched the keyboard or mouse, not what you typed.

    Will it slow my computer down?

    RESUME is built for ordinary laptops with 8 GB of memory. Between screenshots it sleeps, identical screenshots are skipped, and screenshots are compressed. You set a storage limit and how long to keep them.

    What happens when the trial or subscription ends?

    You can still open, search and delete everything you recorded. To start new sessions, choose a plan. Subscriptions keep working offline for 14 days between checks.

    Can I move to a new computer?

    Yes. In RESUME, go to Settings → Plan & license → Release this computer, then paste your key on the new one. Each key works on 2 computers at a time.

    Mac asks for Screen Recording permission.

    RESUME needs it to take screenshots and read window titles. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, turn RESUME on, then reopen the app.
